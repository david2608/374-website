Case study — Icredo · 2026

Borrowing without

Icredo is a banking app for consumer loans. We redesigned application, decisioning and repayment into a single flow a person can finish in one sitting — and understand every step of the way.

01 — The brief

The problem was never the money

Icredo already had the licence, the pricing and the risk model. What it did not have was a flow a person could finish. Applicants dropped out halfway, called support to ask what was happening, and came back a week later to start again from the first screen.

Our brief: one continuous flow from eligibility to first repayment, where the cost of the loan is legible at every step and the state of the application is never a mystery.

“Tell me what it costs, and tell me where I stand.”

Recurring line across 14 applicant interviews

02 — Research

Three findings that reshaped the product

Fourteen applicant interviews, six sessions with credit officers, and a walkthrough of 900 abandoned applications in the existing funnel.

Finding 01

Documents are the cliff edge

Most abandonment happened at the upload step — people were away from their papers, on a phone, with no way to save and return. Nothing about the flow acknowledged that.

Finding 02

Silence reads as rejection

A pending decision with no visible state was interpreted as a no. People stopped waiting and applied elsewhere while their application was still alive.

Finding 03

Trust is built after signing

Repayment was treated as back-office. Yet every question about early payoff, fees and schedule changes arrived there — so that is where the product had to be clearest.

03 — Information architecture

01 / 05

Eligibility in thirty seconds

Four inputs, no account, no credit pull. The applicant sees a real range and the monthly figure before being asked for anything personal.

Documents by camera

Guided capture with instant validation, and a saved draft the moment the first field is filled. Leaving the flow no longer means losing it.

An offer you can compare

Term, rate and total cost side by side, with the trade-off shown as you move the slider. Every fee is named on the same screen as the price.

Signing with a visible state

A live status card replaces the silent wait: what is being checked, what is left, and who to contact — carried into notifications.

Repayment you can steer

Schedule, early payoff quote and payment-date change in one place — the three things people used to call support about.
