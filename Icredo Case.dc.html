02 The Challenges

The Complexity was Everywhere.

The existing credit journey was fragmented across digital and physical touchpoints, making it difficult to complete, understand, and navigate.
The challenge was to bring the experience together into a faster, clearer, and more accessible mobile journey.

Connecting Every Step

Making the digital journey as complete as the credit journey itself.

Where Digital Credit Fell Short

The existing website allowed users to apply for credit online, but the experience still depended on visiting a branch to complete the process.

One Place for Your Banking Journey

The new app needed to bring more of the journey into one place while keeping the flexibility to interact with a physical branch when needed.

Clarity at Every Step

Users had to understand their options, choose a loan amount and repayment period, and move through the process with confidence.

The challenge was to make a naturally complex financial journey feel faster, more accessible, and easier to navigate.

03 Research & Our Approach

We Started with The Users Already Choosing iCredo.

By analyzing user data and audience segments, we identified a predominantly younger, digitally active user base.

That insight shaped the direction of the product.

Instead of recreating the traditional form-based credit experience on mobile, we explored a more interactive and conversational approach.

04 Solution

More Flexible Way to Access Credit.

The final app brings the credit journey into a conversational mobile experience.

Users can choose how much they want to borrow and how long they want to repay it through chat, with the product guiding them through the process.

They can then receive their funds digitally or choose to visit a branch, depending on what works best for them.

By combining digital access with offline flexibility, the product gives users more control over how they interact with the service.
