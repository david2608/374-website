Case study · Consumer lending

Credit you can actually read.

We rebuilt Icredo’s loan app so that every rate, fee and deadline explains itself before the customer signs — one flow, three markets, no legal fog.

Platforms

Timeline

01 — Overview

Borrowing money is a moment of doubt. The interface should be the calm part.

Icredo lends to salaried customers across three markets. The product worked — approvals were fast, pricing was fair — but the app spoke in the voice of a compliance department. People abandoned applications not because the offer was bad, but because they could not tell what they had agreed to.

Our brief was narrow on purpose: keep every regulated disclosure, change nothing in the credit engine, and make the whole thing legible to a person reading it on a bus.

02 — Context & challenge

Applications died in the middle, not at the price.

Session replays showed the same shape every time: confident start, long pause on the disclosure step, exit. Support calls asked one question in a hundred ways — what will this actually cost me?

Constraint

Nine disclosures, none optional

Regulators define what must appear and when. We could restructure and rewrite, never remove.

Constraint

A core banking system from 2011

Offer data arrives in batches. The interface had to stay honest about what it did not yet know.

Constraint

Three markets, one flow

Different rate caps and document rules, one product team. Variation had to live in tokens and content, not forks.

04 — Key decisions

Three moves carried the redesign.

Decision 01

One number, always in view.

Total cost of credit lives in a pinned summary that updates as the customer moves the amount and term. Nothing is revealed at signature that was hidden at step one.

Monthly payment, total repayable and APR in one row

Estimates labelled as estimates until the core system confirms

Insurance and fees shown as add-ons, never folded into the headline

Decision 02

Disclosures that read like sentences.

We kept all nine regulated statements and rewrote their framing: a plain-language summary first, the legal text one tap below, and a checklist that tells the customer exactly what they are confirming.

Legal copy reviewed line by line with compliance, twice

Reading level dropped from B2 to A2 across the flow

Market-specific clauses handled by content tokens

Decision 03

Approval is a status, not a wait.

Because decisions arrive in batches, the app now narrates its own state — what has been checked, what is pending, what the customer can do meanwhile. The blank spinner is gone.

Five named application states with honest timing

Document upload can continue while scoring runs

Push and in-app status always agree
